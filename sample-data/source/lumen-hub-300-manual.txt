Lumen Hub 300 - User Manual
Smart home controller by Corvid Devices. Manual revision C, firmware 3.8.

The Lumen Hub 300, Corvid Devices and everything in this manual are fictional sample data written
for DocQuery. They do not describe a real product.

1. Overview
The Lumen Hub 300 connects your lights, sensors, locks and thermostats and lets you control them
from the Lumen app, even when you are away from home. Automations run on the hub itself, so they
keep working during an internet outage.

2. In the box
- Lumen Hub 300
- USB-C power adapter (5 V, 2 A) and 1.5 m cable
- Wall mounting plate and two screws
- Quick start card

3. Technical specifications
Radios: Zigbee 3.0, Thread 1.3 and Wi-Fi 6 (2.4 and 5 GHz). Bluetooth LE is used for setup only.
Capacity: up to 128 paired devices.
Power: 5 V, 2 A over USB-C. Typical consumption: 1.8 W.
Size and weight: 98 x 98 x 24 mm, 180 g.
Operating conditions: 0 to 40 degrees Celsius, indoor use only, 10 to 85 % humidity.

4. Setting up the hub
1. Download the Lumen app and create an account.
2. Plug in the hub. The status light pulses white while it starts, which takes about one minute.
3. In the app, tap Add hub and scan the QR code on the bottom of the hub.
4. Choose your Wi-Fi network. The hub only joins networks protected with WPA2 or WPA3.
Pairing mode for new devices lasts 120 seconds. To start it again, press the pair button once.

5. Status light
- Solid green: the hub is online and working normally.
- Pulsing white: the hub is starting.
- Pulsing blue: pairing mode is on.
- Pulsing amber: the hub is not set up yet or is waiting for Wi-Fi details.
- Solid red: the hub has no network connection.
- Flashing red: a firmware update failed (see error E37).

6. Resetting the hub
To restart the hub, hold the reset button for 10 seconds; your settings are kept.
To restore factory settings, hold the reset button for 30 seconds until the light turns amber.
A factory reset removes all paired devices and automations.

7. Troubleshooting and error codes
Error codes are shown in the Lumen app under Hub > Diagnostics.

E14 - Zigbee radio not responding. Unplug the hub for 30 seconds and plug it in again. If the error
comes back, contact Corvid support; the hub may need to be replaced under warranty.

E21 - Wi-Fi authentication failed. The Wi-Fi password was rejected. Check the password in the app,
then make sure the network uses WPA2 or WPA3.

E37 - Firmware signature invalid. The downloaded update could not be verified and was not installed.
Do not unplug the hub. It restores the previous firmware automatically and retries the update after
one hour. If E37 appears three times in a row, contact Corvid support.

E52 - Device limit reached. The hub already has 128 paired devices. Remove devices you no longer use
before pairing new ones.

Devices drop off the network: move the hub to a central, raised position away from metal objects and
microwave ovens, and add mains-powered Zigbee devices, which act as repeaters.

8. Updates
Firmware updates are released about once a month and install automatically between 02:00 and 05:00
local time. You can postpone an update by up to 7 days in the app.

9. Warranty
The Lumen Hub 300 has a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. If you register the hub
in the Lumen app within 30 days of purchase, the warranty is extended to 3 years. The warranty does
not cover water damage, damage caused by opening the case, or use with a power adapter other than
the one supplied.

10. Support
Corvid support is available by chat in the Lumen app, Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 20:00.
Have your hub's serial number ready; it is printed next to the QR code.

11. Safety
Do not cover the hub or install it near a heat source. Keep it away from water. Children should not
play with the hub or its cable.
