# Brightwell Analytics — Information Classification and Data Retention Policy

*Policy owner: Daniel Okoro, Chief Information Security Officer. Approved by the Board on 12 January 2026.
Reviewed every year.*

> Fictional sample data written for DocQuery. Brightwell Analytics and the people named here do not exist.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how Brightwell classifies information and how long it keeps it. It applies to all
employees, contractors and suppliers who handle Brightwell information, in any format and on any device.

## 2. Classification levels

| Level | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Marketing material, published reports | No restrictions |
| Internal | Handbook, team plans, internal wikis | Employees and contractors only |
| Confidential | Customer data, contracts, salaries | Need-to-know access, encrypted in transit and at rest |
| Restricted | Security keys, incident reports, M&A plans | Named individuals only, approved by the CISO |

When in doubt, classify information as **Confidential** until its owner decides otherwise.

## 3. Retention periods

- Customer data is deleted **90 days after the end of the customer's contract**, unless the customer asks for earlier deletion.
- Access and audit logs are kept for **13 months**.
- Invoices and accounting records are kept for **7 years**, as required by law.
- CVs and interview notes of unsuccessful candidates are deleted **6 months** after the hiring decision.
- Backups are kept for **35 days** and then overwritten.

## 4. Passwords and access

- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long. Passphrases are encouraged.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for every company system that supports it.
- Access rights are reviewed every quarter by each system owner.

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be requested in writing and approved by the CISO. Approved exceptions
are recorded in the risk register and expire after 12 months.
